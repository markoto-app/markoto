Kiln energy audit

Aldervale Ceramics, main workshop. Audit carried out by an external engineer in September 2026.

Scope

The audit measured the gas and electricity use of the workshop's three kilns over twelve months (October 2025 to September 2026) and compared each kiln with the manufacturer's rated consumption.

Findings

  • The three kilns used 212 MWh of energy in the twelve months, 64 % of the workshop's total.
  • Kiln two used 38 % more energy per firing than its rated consumption, because its door seal has worn and its lid insulation has cracked.
  • Kilns one and three were within 5 % of their ratings.
  • Firings started on weekday mornings ran at the highest tariff; moving them to the night tariff would cut costs without changing output.
