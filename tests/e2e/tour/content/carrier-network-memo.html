Carrier network: Q3 2026 memo

Quaybridge Software GmbH, Network team. Internal memo to the management board, 14 October 2026.

Volume

Carriers booked 2.84 million dock appointments in Q3 2026, up from 2.17 million in Q3 2025. 9,400 carriers booked at least one appointment in the quarter (Q3 2025: 7,100). In September alone 1,150 carriers registered for the first time, most of them through invitations sent by the three grocery distribution centres that went live in July.

Slot Forecast sites

Slot Forecast was live at 64 sites at the end of September. We compared the weeks after each site's go-live (19 August to 30 September) with the same weeks before it:

  • The no-show rate, appointments where the truck did not arrive within its window, fell from 11.2% to 7.9%.
  • Average truck waiting time at the gate fell from 41 minutes to 28 minutes.
  • Sites that also use the carrier app 3.0 saw the largest drop in no-shows.

Fees

The average fee stayed at EUR 0.30 per appointment. We propose no change to the fee in 2027.

Next quarter

We will pilot capacity pooling at three distribution centres in Q1 2027 and report the first results to the board in April.
