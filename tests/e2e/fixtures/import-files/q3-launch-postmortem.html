Q3 launch postmortem

Date: 2026-04-12
Attendees: Maya Chen (PM), Jordan Lee (Eng), Sam Reeves (Design)
Purpose: Capture what went well + what we'll do differently for Q4.

What worked

  • Staged rollout caught the avatar-loading regression at 5% before user reports landed.
  • The on-call rotation handed off cleanly; no incidents required escalation past tier-1.
  • Customer-success ran the launch FAQ on a separate doc, freed the engineering channel for actual work.

What we'll change

  • Cut the staged-rollout interval from 24h to 12h — the avatar bug spent half its window invisible behind a metric we don't watch.
  • Add a "blast radius" line to every PR description. Two of the three near-misses were features we underestimated.
  • Move the launch retro to T+5 days, not T+10. Memories rot.

Open questions

  1. Should the on-call rotation include a designer? Two of our incidents this quarter were UI regressions.
  2. Who owns the canary metric dashboard now that Mira's team moved off it?
