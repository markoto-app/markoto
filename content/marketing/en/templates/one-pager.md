# One-pager template in Markdown

A one-pager is read in two minutes by someone who has not met you: an investor, a partner, a manager who forwards it. It has to fit one page. This template has the five parts that make one work, short enough that you cut text rather than shrink the type.

Below is a one-pager for a made-up product. The preview shows the single page it prints on.

::try-it{theme="one-pager" height="760"}
---
source: |
  # Tidal

  **Tidal tells small harbours when a berth frees up, so visiting boats stop circling and harbour masters stop answering the phone.**

  ## The problem

  Harbour masters on the coast take 40 to 60 calls a day in summer asking whether a berth is free. Skippers cruise from harbour to harbour to find out.

  ## The solution

  - A live berth map each harbour updates in one tap.
  - Skippers see free berths on their phone, with no account.

  ## How it works

  1. The harbour master marks a berth free or taken.
  2. The map updates for every skipper nearby.
  3. The skipper reserves for the night or just arrives.

  ## Key facts

  | Metric | Before | After |
  |---|---:|---:|
  | Calls per day | 52 | 11 |
  | Empty berths at 6 pm | 9 | 3 |

  > [!IMPORTANT]
  > The pilot ran in three harbours for one summer and cost the harbours nothing.

  ## Next steps

  1. Join the pilot for summer 2027.
  2. Introduce us to your harbour association.

  **Pricing:** Free for harbours. **Team:** Three people. **Contact:** hello@example.org
---

## What's in it

- **Title block and pitch.** The title and a subtitle in the block above, and one bold sentence: what it is, for whom, and what changes for them.
- **The problem.** Two or three sentences on the pain your reader knows, with a number if you have a sourced one.
- **The solution.** What it does for the reader, and up to three bullets with the properties that matter most.
- **How it works.** Three numbered steps from the user's point of view.
- **Key facts.** A small table with the three or four numbers that prove it works, and a callout for the one thing the reader must not miss.
- **Next steps.** What you ask the reader to do, then price, team and contact in one line.

The briefs under the headings are Markdown comments: visible in the editor and to the AI assistant, never on the page or in the PDF.

## Keeping it to one page

The preview shows the page break as you type, so you see at once when a line spills onto a second page. Cut a sentence instead of shrinking the font. The page count is in the status bar of the editor.

## Sending it

Download the PDF to attach to an email, or share the document as a read-only link. A published web page is another option: it opens in any browser at its own address and works on a phone. Each reader sees the page as it was when you published it.
