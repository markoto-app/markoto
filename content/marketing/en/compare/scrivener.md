# markoto vs Scrivener

Scrivener and markoto can both hold a novel, but they are built for different parts of the work.

**Scrivener** is a desktop application for long writing projects. You break the book into sections, arrange them on a corkboard or in an outline, keep research next to the draft, set word targets, and compile the result to Word, PDF, ebook and other formats.

**markoto** is a collaborative Markdown editor in the browser. You write the book as text, the preview shows it as manuscript pages, readers comment on passages, and the PDF matches what you see. It has no planning tools of Scrivener's kind.

## Where Scrivener is better

- **Planning.** The corkboard, the outliner and sections you drag around are made for structuring a book. markoto has headings and an AI that can move chapters, but no index cards.
- **Research beside the draft.** Scrivener keeps your notes, PDFs and images in the project and shows several documents side by side. markoto's research feature is a workspace Knowledge tab you query through the AI.
- **Targets.** Word and character targets for the whole book, a section or a session. markoto counts words in the status bar and has no goals.
- **Compile.** Scrivener's compile step turns one project into a Word file, a PDF, an ebook or a script. markoto produces a PDF, a Markdown file or a web page.
- **A native app.** Scrivener is installed on your computer, tablet or phone. markoto needs a browser and a connection.
- **Long-form tools.** Snapshots of a section, labels, status fields, keywords and collections are part of the design of a book-length project.

## Where markoto is better

- **Runs in a browser.** Nothing to install, any device, and a link to send to someone else.
- **Comments from readers.** Beta readers comment on the passages they mean, you reply and resolve, in the same document. Scrivener's own pages don't describe a feature for this; two authors merge copies of a project.
- **Live co-writing.** Two people can write in the same document at once.
- **A PDF that matches.** The preview shows pages as you type, and the PDF is those pages.
- **Edit-level history.** Every change is recorded and any earlier state can be restored, not only the sections you remembered to snapshot.
- **An AI editor you control.** It rewrites a selected passage, answers a question in a comment and answers from files you uploaded, when you ask. Every change can be undone.
- **Price and hosting.** Free on markoto.app, hosted in the EU and operated by a Swiss company, or self-hosted.

## Who should pick which

Pick **Scrivener** if you are drafting a long book alone, want to plan it on a corkboard, and need to compile to Word or an ebook for a publisher.

Pick **markoto** if you want to write in the browser, share chapters and collect comments, work with a co-author or an editor, and end with a PDF to read or send. Some writers plan and draft in a dedicated tool and move chapters into markoto for review.

## Moving between them

Scrivener can export text, so you can paste a chapter into markoto as Markdown. Going the other way, a markoto document downloads as a Markdown file you can import into other tools. There is no direct import of a Scrivener project, and none of Scrivener's metadata, such as labels or snapshots, comes with the text.
