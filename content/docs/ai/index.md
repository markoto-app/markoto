# AI assistant

The AI works inside your document: it writes, rewrites and looks things
up, and you see its edits as they happen. Start with the overview to see
what each feature needs. AI features require an account.

**Start here**
- [What the AI can do, and what it needs](/docs/ai/overview): features, providers, limits.
- [Write with the AI](/docs/ai/prompt-bar): the prompt bar walkthrough.
- [Rewrite a selection with the AI](/docs/ai/rewrite-selection): highlight, prompt, replace.
- [Summon the AI from a comment](/docs/ai/comments): `@markoto` mention in any thread.
- [Tell the AI who you're writing for](/docs/ai/document-context): audience + language steering.
- [Keep a project bible for the AI](/docs/ai/project-context): characters, glossary, style guide in every request.

**Large documents and large edits**
- [Work with long documents](/docs/ai/long-documents): ask about one section, rename a term everywhere.
- [Translate or restyle a whole document](/docs/ai/document-jobs): background edits you can cancel, resume and undo as a whole.

**Research and sources**
- [Ask the AI for current information](/docs/ai/web-search): grounded answers from live web results.
- [Ask the AI to read a URL](/docs/ai/fetch-url): pull in any external page.
- [Ask the AI about another doc](/docs/ai/cross-doc): cross-doc retrieval.
- [Ask the AI about a file you uploaded](/docs/ai/knowledge-rag): workspace knowledge retrieval.
- [Ask the AI to draw an image](/docs/ai/generate-image): generate, upload, insert.
