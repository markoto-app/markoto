# Upgrade your plan

> **Availability.** Paid plans are **coming soon** on markoto.app, so there is
> nothing to buy yet: no checkout, no billing portal. Self-hosted instances
> never have paid plans. This page describes the flow for instances where
> paid plans are switched on.

Where plans are live, open the [pricing page](/pricing) (the **Upgrade**
buttons in the AI bar and the usage chip lead there). Tick the box that
accepts the [Paid Plans Terms](/legal/paid-terms) and asks for the plan to
start right away, then press **Upgrade** on Pro and you land in Stripe
Checkout. Pay, and the new plan is active in your account as soon as
Stripe confirms the payment, usually before you are back on the markoto tab.
Managing or cancelling a subscription opens Stripe's billing portal. Changes
take effect as Stripe reports them; if a subscription lapses, you fall back
to the free tier and nothing is deleted.

On Pro you can also buy **AI credits** under Settings → Profile → Spending
and usage (10, 25 or 50, in euros or Swiss francs; tick the Paid Plans
Terms box first). Credits top up the
monthly AI budget: they are used only after the month's budget is spent,
and they expire 12 months after purchase. A refunded purchase removes the
refunded share of its credits.

The checkout and portal links answer "billing is disabled" wherever billing
is off.
