# Upgrade your plan

> **Availability.** Paid plans are on sale on markoto.app. Self-hosted
> instances have no paid plans.

Open the [pricing page](/pricing) (the **Upgrade** buttons in the AI bar
and the usage chip lead there). Tick the box that accepts the
[Paid Plans Terms](/legal/paid-terms) and asks for the plan to start right
away, then press **Upgrade** on Pro and you land in Stripe Checkout. On
markoto.app you buy from **Link**, a Stripe company, which sells the plan
as merchant of record: it charges the VAT, issues the invoices and
receipts and handles refunds. Pay, and the new plan is active in your
account as soon as the payment is confirmed, usually before you are back on
the markoto tab. Managing or cancelling a subscription opens Stripe's
billing portal; you can also cancel in your Link account. Changes take
effect as they are reported back; if a subscription lapses, you fall back
to the free tier and nothing is deleted.

Extra AI Points on top of the monthly allowance can't be bought on
markoto.app yet.

On an instance where billing is off, the checkout and portal links answer
"billing is disabled".
