# Invite a teammate to your workspace

Open the members panel on the workspace page, type your teammate's email
address, pick a role and click **Invite**. Nobody joins your workspace
without saying yes: your teammate gets an invitation and becomes a member
only once they accept it.

Invites are exact-email: markoto doesn't suggest or look up names as you
type, so nobody can find out who has an account by typing letters into
the box.

## How your teammate accepts

- **In markoto.** When they sign in with the email address you invited,
  the invitation shows up under **Invitations** on their dashboard, with
  the workspace name, the role and who invited them. They click
  **Accept** (or **Decline**).
- **By email.** If email is set up on this markoto instance, they also
  get an invitation email with a link. The link works once, and they can
  use it even if they sign in with a different address.

Invitations expire after 14 days. Inviting the same address again
replaces the open invitation, with the new role and a fresh 14 days.

## Managing open invitations

As a workspace admin you see open invitations below the member list:
the address you typed, the role and when the invitation expires. Click
the **×** next to one to revoke it. A workspace can have up to 50 open
invitations at a time, and there's an hourly limit on how many you can
send.
