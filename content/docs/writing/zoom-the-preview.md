# Zoom the preview and see all pages

The preview shows your document as the pages it will print on. Normally one
page fills the width of the preview. When you want to check the small print,
zoom in; when you want to see where the chapters start and the tables land,
step back and look at every page at once.

This walkthrough zooms into a long report, pulls back to all pages and
opens one of them again. About a minute.

::flow{id="zoom-the-preview" hide-title}
::

## What you can do

- **Fit width** is the default: one page as wide as the preview.
- **Zoom in and out** with the **+** and **−** buttons in the top right
  corner of the preview, or pick a fixed size from 50% to 200% in the zoom
  menu between them.
- **All pages** (the grid button) lays the pages out side by side, as many
  to a row as fit. Click any page to open it at full width.
- **From the keyboard:** with the pointer over the preview, press
  <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and <kbd>+</kbd> to zoom in, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and
  <kbd>−</kbd> to zoom out, and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and <kbd>0</kbd> to go back
  to fit width. On a Mac, use <kbd>Cmd</kbd>. Zooming out past 50% shows
  all pages.
- **Pinch** on a trackpad, or hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and scroll, for any size
  in between. On a phone, pinch the preview with two fingers; pinching all
  the way out shows all pages.
- The zoom options are also under **View › Zoom**.

Zoom only changes how you look at the document. Your PDF, the pages, and
what your collaborators see stay exactly the same. markoto remembers your
choice in this browser and uses it for every document you open.

On a phone, switch to the preview with the eye button at the top right; the
zoom button sits right next to it.
