# Sign out everywhere

Signed in on a computer you no longer use, or worried someone else has
access to your account? Open your profile (`/settings/profile`) and click
**Log out everywhere** in the **Sessions** section. markoto signs you out
in every browser and on every device, including the one you're using.
Sign in again wherever you want to keep working.

Requesting account deletion also signs you out on every other device;
the browser you used to request it stays signed in, so you can still
cancel the deletion.

## Why markoto sometimes asks you to sign in again

A few actions need a recent sign-in, from the last 12 hours:

- deleting your account,
- requesting a data export,
- saving or removing your own AI provider settings.

If your sign-in is older, markoto shows **Sign in again**. Click it, sign
in with your usual provider, and repeat the action. This protects your
account if someone gets hold of a browser where you're still signed in.
