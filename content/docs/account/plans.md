# Plans and limits

The free tier includes anonymous and signed-in documents, every
theme, every markdown extension, collaboration, comments, history and
restore, PDF export, workspaces, import, the MCP server, and all the AI
features (with your own AI provider). On self-hosted instances and on
markoto.app today every feature is on; once paid plans are live, plans set
limits and a few features (listed below).

What you see depends on where markoto runs:

| Where | Plans |
|---|---|
| **markoto.app** | Everyone is on Free. The [pricing page](/pricing) shows one card per plan: Free, Pro, Team and Business. Before you sign in it also shows the two ways to use markoto without an account: self-hosted and without signing up. Pro, Team and Business are marked **coming soon**: they show the prices we plan to charge (in euros or Swiss francs, monthly or annual, per seat for Team and Business; not final) and no purchase button. You can join a waitlist (double opt-in by email; you can unsubscribe at any time) to hear when they launch. |
| **A self-hosted instance** | No plans, no pricing page tiers, no waitlist, no purchase. Everyone is on the free tier, and the operator decides the limits (workspaces, knowledge storage, AI Points). |
| **An instance with paid plans live** (later) | Free and Pro for individuals with Stripe checkout ([Upgrade](/docs/account/upgrade)); Team and Business for workspaces, billed per seat. |

## What limits a plan sets

- **Workspaces you can own.** Workspaces you are invited to never count.
- **Knowledge storage**: files and bytes per workspace and per person ([knowledge](/docs/writing/knowledge-rag)).
- **Image storage**: the total size of the images you upload or the AI generates for you, across all your documents (100 MB on the free plan).
- **AI Points**, the monthly allowance for AI that the operator pays for. Calls on your own AI provider are not limited by it ([AI Points](/docs/writing/ai-budget)).
- **Team and Business** (once available): a workspace subscription billed per seat (at least 3), with AI budget and storage shared by its members. The AI budget and storage limits on the pricing page are per seat: a 5-seat Team workspace gets five times the per-seat amount, shared. See [Team and Business](/docs/account/team-plan).

## What features a plan includes

On markoto.app some features depend on the plan once paid plans are live:
custom CSS in themes, how far back history and restore reach, MCP write
access, and the AI image generation, voice input and the stronger AI model
that markoto pays for. Features a plan doesn't include show as locked
controls with a short note. **Your own AI provider is never limited by the
plan**: image generation, voice and any model work with your own key on
every plan.

Inside a Team or Business workspace, its plan applies to you, whatever your
own plan is. Your profile lists the workspaces that cover you, and documents
in such a workspace show the plan next to their title. Moving a document
out of the workspace pauses the features the new place's plan doesn't
include; nothing is deleted.

## AI credits

When a month's AI Points run out early, Pro users and Team / Business
workspace admins can buy AI credits. Credits are used only after the
month's plan allowance is spent, carry over from month to month and expire
12 months after purchase. The free plan can't buy credits.

## Seeing your usage

To see how much of each limit you use, click the usage chip in the top bar
(the AI Points meter, or **Your own AI provider**). Under **Storage & limits**
it lists your images, knowledge files, knowledge storage and the workspaces
you own, each as used / limit. A bar turns orange at three quarters full and
red when you are close to the limit. Your profile page shows your image
storage too.

The walkthrough above shows the pricing page on markoto.app, with paid plans
coming soon. Under the cards, a table compares every limit for each option. The current numbers always come from the instance you are on:
open its pricing page to see them.
