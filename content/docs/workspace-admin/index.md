# Workspace administration

These pages are for workspace admins. They cover the settings that
apply to the whole workspace and everyone in it. On markoto.app some of
them need a Team or Business plan; each page says which.

- [Brand kit of a workspace](/docs/workspace-admin/brand-kit): your fonts and logos, a locked design, templates for members.
- [Single sign-on for a workspace](/docs/workspace-admin/single-sign-on): OpenID Connect or SAML, domain verification, SCIM provisioning.
- [Audit log of a workspace](/docs/workspace-admin/audit-log): see and download who changed what.

**Elsewhere**
- [Team and Business for a workspace](/docs/plans/team-plan), buying and managing seats, lives in Plans and billing.
- Members, roles and guests are under [Workspaces](/docs/workspaces).
