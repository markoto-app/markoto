# See what changed in past edits

Every edit to a doc lands as a row in the activity log: title
changes, theme changes, content edits, comments, restores. Click any
edit row and a diff drawer opens with hunk-level highlights:
red strikethroughs for what came out, green for what went in.

::flow{id="see-what-changed-in-past-edits" hide-title}
::

## What just happened

markoto compares the document just before and just after that edit and
shows the changed passages with their author's colour, line numbers and
a little surrounding context.

Very large changes (pasting over the whole document, for example) are
cut off after 200 changed passages; to see the full earlier text, use
[restore](/docs/import-export/restore).
