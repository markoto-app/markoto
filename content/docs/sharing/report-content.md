# Report a document

Anyone can write on markoto without an account, and markoto doesn't
check what people write. Documents created without an account show a
short note at the top: **Created by a markoto user without an account,
not by markoto**. If a link you were sent contains a scam, illegal
content or spam, use **Report** in that note.

::flow{id="report-a-document" hide-title}
::

## What to put in a report

- **The address of the document.** The Report link fills it in for you.
  You can also open the form from the footer and paste the link.
- **What is wrong with it.** Say which part is illegal or breaks the
  rules, and why. The more specific, the faster we can act.
- **Your name and email.** We send you a confirmation and, later, our
  decision. For reports of child sexual abuse material you can leave
  them out, but then we can't tell you what we decided.
- **A short confirmation** that the report is accurate and made in good
  faith.

You don't need an account. A quick automatic check in your browser
makes sure you're a person; it doesn't track you.

## What happens next

We review every report. When we remove or restrict a document, its
owner gets an email explaining what we did, which rule it broke and how
to object. Documents created without an account have no owner to write
to.

See the [abuse and illegal-content policy](/legal/abuse-policy) for what
we act on.
