# Share

markoto supports four access modes per document (private, open-edit,
open-view, and password-protected) plus workspace-scoped roles
(admin / editor / viewer) for team collaboration.

- [Write & share your first doc](/docs/getting-started/quick-share): anon doc, share by URL, copy to clipboard.
- [Share as read-only](/docs/sharing/read-only): open-view mode; anyone with the link reads, nobody edits.
- [Lock a document behind a password](/docs/sharing/password-protect): set a passphrase, gate the share link.
- [Share by QR code](/docs/sharing/qr-code): encode the link for in-the-room sharing.
- [Link expiry and view-only links](/docs/sharing/link-settings): let a link stop working on a date, turn off download and print.
- [Publish as a web page](/docs/sharing/publish-as-a-web-page): a public, read-only copy at its own address, optionally on your own domain.
- [Report a document](/docs/sharing/report-content): report a scam, illegal content or spam, no account needed.
