# Link expiry and view-only links

> **Availability.** On markoto.app link settings are part of the **Pro**,
> **Team** and **Business** plans. On a self-hosted instance
> every document has them.

When a document is shared by link (anyone can view, anyone can edit, or with
a password), its owner and the workspace's admins find **Link settings** in
the share dialog.

::flow{id="limit-a-share-link" hide-title}
::

## Let the link stop working

Choose the date and time the link **stops working**. From then on, only you
and the members of the workspace can open the document; everyone else sees
"This link has expired". Remove the date to keep the link working again.
People who have the document open at that moment can keep reading it for up
to two hours.

## View only: no download or print

Turn on **View only: no download or print** and people with the link can
read the document, but File → Export, Save as PDF and Print are gone for
them, and markoto refuses to make a PDF for them. You and the workspace's
members can still download and print. This keeps a document from being
passed around as a file; it can't stop anyone from copying text they can
read on screen.

## How often the link was opened

Under the settings you see how often the document was opened in the last
30 days by people other than you and the workspace's members, and when it
was last opened. markoto counts opens per day only, not who opened the
link, from where or how often each person did.
