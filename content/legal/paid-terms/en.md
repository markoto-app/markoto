# Paid Plans Terms

> **In short:** these terms add to the Terms of Service when you buy Pro, Team, Business or AI credits. You buy from **Link** (Sold through Link, LLC, a Stripe company), which resells markoto as merchant of record: Link takes the payment, handles VAT, sends the invoice and decides on refunds under its buyer terms. Plans renew every month or year until you cancel; cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep the plan until the period ends. Team and Business are bought for a workspace and billed per seat, with at least 3 seats. AI credits top up the monthly AI budget, are used only after the plan's budget is spent, and expire 12 months after purchase. EU and Swiss consumers keep their statutory rights; if you ask for the plan to start right away, a withdrawal within 14 days costs the share of the period you used.

## 2. Plans

- **Pro** is a personal plan. It applies to you: your documents, your workspaces and your AI use outside Team and Business workspaces.
- **Team** and **Business** are bought for one workspace. They apply inside that workspace, to every member, whatever their own plan is. The person who buys is the workspace's **billing admin**; another workspace admin can take over that role.
- **Seats.** Team and Business are billed per seat, with at least **3 seats**. Members and open invitations each use a seat. Adding a seat beyond the paid number raises the subscription and charges the share of the current period right away; the added seat's AI budget likewise covers only the days left in the current monthly budget period. Removing seats gives no credit for the current period; the removed seats' AI budget stays until the budget period ends, and the next invoice bills the new number.
- **What a plan includes** is the list on the pricing page at the time you buy or renew. Limits that are given per seat (AI budget, knowledge and image storage) are pooled for the whole workspace.

## 3. Prices, payment and taxes

- Prices are set in euros or Swiss francs, monthly or annual, as shown on the pricing page and at checkout, and include VAT where it applies. Checkout may also show the price converted into your local currency; the amount you pay is the one shown at checkout. Your first purchase fixes the currency of your account (or of the workspace); it can't be changed later.
- Link calculates the VAT or sales tax for your country at checkout. If you buy as a business, you can enter your VAT number at checkout; where the reverse-charge rules apply, Link charges no VAT and its invoice says so.
- You pay Link in advance for each period, with one of the payment methods checkout offers. Your bank or card statement shows the purchase as from Link (for example "LINK.COM* MARKOTO"). We never see your full card details.
- By buying a subscription you allow Link to charge your payment method at every renewal until you cancel. If a renewal payment fails, the payment is retried for a few days and you are told by email. The plan stays active while the payment is retried; if it finally fails, the subscription ends and the account or workspace returns to the free plan.
- We may change prices for future periods. You are told at least **30 days** before a change takes effect for you, and you can cancel before it does.

## 4. Renewal and cancellation

- Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each month or year until you cancel.
- You can cancel at any time in the billing settings (the Stripe customer portal) for Pro, or in the workspace's Billing tab for Team and Business, or in your Link account at link.com. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the plan stays active until the end of the period already paid. Periods already paid are not refunded, except as stated in §6 or where Link grants a refund under its buyer terms.
- **Deleting a workspace** with a Team or Business plan ends its subscription at once; the unused share of the period is kept as a credit on the workspace's customer account, not refunded. Ask us at **{{privacyContactEmail}}** if you need a refund instead; we ask Link to issue it.
- **Deleting your account** ends your Pro plan at once. A Team or Business subscription you pay passes to the workspace's longest-serving other admin, or ends at the end of the period if there is none.

## 5. AI budget and AI credits

- Each plan has a **monthly AI budget**, shown as AI Points (1,000 points = 1 US dollar of AI costs at our provider prices). It resets every month and doesn't carry over. In a Team or Business workspace the budget is shared; workspace admins can set a limit per member.
- Pro users and Team or Business workspace admins can buy **AI credits** in fixed packs. Credits are used only after the month's plan budget is spent. Unused credits carry over from month to month and expire **12 months** after purchase. Credits are only usable while a paid plan is active; they are not money, can't be paid out and can't be moved to another account or workspace.
- AI used through your own AI provider (bring your own key) never uses your budget or credits.

## 6. Refunds and right of withdrawal

**Refunds.** Link decides on refund requests under its buyer terms ([link.com/terms](https://link.com/terms)); ask Link through your Link account or its support, or ask us at **{{privacyContactEmail}}** and we pass it on. A refunded credit pack loses the refunded share of its credits (credits already used stay used); a plan whose subscription is cancelled with a refund ends at that time.

**Right of withdrawal.** If you are a consumer living in the EU, the EEA, Switzerland or the United Kingdom and the law gives you a right to withdraw from a purchase within **14 days** without giving a reason, you exercise it towards Link as the seller, as described in Link's buyer terms. You can also tell us clearly at **{{privacyContactEmail}}** within the 14 days, and we pass it on to Link.

- **Plans.** By ticking the box at checkout, you ask for your plan to start right away. If you then withdraw within 14 days, you get back what you paid minus the share for the time the plan was active.
- **AI credits.** By ticking the box at checkout, you agree that the credits are available right away and that your right of withdrawal ends once you start using them. Unused credits can be withdrawn within 14 days for a full refund.

Other statutory consumer rights, under EU, Swiss or UK law, are not limited by these terms. Business users have no right of withdrawal.

## 7. Changes to plans

- **Switching** a workspace between Team and Business, or between monthly and annual billing, takes effect once the share of the price difference for the rest of the period is paid; it is charged right away. If your bank asks you to confirm the payment, the switch waits until you do; if the payment isn't completed within about a day, nothing changes. The same applies to added seats.
- **Ending a plan** (cancelling) returns the account or workspace to the free plan at the end of the period. Nothing is deleted: content above the free plan's limits stays, but you can't add more until you are below the limit again, and features the free plan doesn't include pause (for example custom CSS, older history, or more members than the plan allows). The same applies to a document moved out of a Team or Business workspace.
- We may change what a plan includes. If a change materially reduces what you paid for, we tell you at least **30 days** in advance, and you can cancel with a refund of the unused share of the current period.

Link sends receipts and invoices, in its own name as the seller, to the email address you entered at checkout; they are also available in the customer portal and your Link account. We keep billing records for the period required by tax law (in Switzerland 10 years). How we and Link handle billing data is described in the Privacy Policy ([/legal/privacy](/legal/privacy)).
