# Abuse and Illegal Content Policy

> **In short:** Anyone, with or without an account, can report content on markoto that they believe is illegal or breaks our Terms. We confirm receipt, a person reviews every report, and we tell you what we decided. If we remove someone's content, we tell them why and how to object. We report threats to life or safety to the authorities.

## What this policy covers

This policy is our **notice-and-action mechanism** under Article 16 of the EU **Digital Services Act** (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, "DSA"). It also describes our statements of reasons (Art. 17), how affected users can object, and when we notify authorities (Art. 18). The rules on what is allowed are in §6 of our Terms of Service ([/legal/terms](/legal/terms)), and how we moderate is in §8.

## How to report

You can report content in two ways. Both are handled the same way:

- **The report form** at [/legal/abuse](/legal/abuse). It works without an account; your browser then solves a short bot check that runs on our own server, with no third party and no cookie. Anonymous documents (documents created without an account) show a bar reading "Created by a markoto user without an account, not by markoto" with a "Report" link. The link opens the form with the document's address filled in.
- **Email** to **{{abuseContactEmail}}**.

A complete report contains (Art. 16(2) DSA):

1. **The exact location** of the content: the document's URL (for example `https://markoto.app/d/…`) and, if useful, which part of the document you mean.
2. **An explanation** of why you believe the content is illegal or breaks our Terms. If it is illegal, say which law it breaks, if you know.
3. **Your name and email address.** You may leave these out only when you report child sexual abuse material.
4. **A statement** that you believe, in good faith, that the information and claims in your report are accurate and complete.

Reports that are incomplete may take longer, because we may not be able to identify the content or judge it.

**Do not send us copies of child sexual abuse material**, not even as evidence. Send only the URL. You can also report such material directly to the police or to a hotline of the INHOPE network.

## What happens after you report

1. **Receipt.** We confirm receipt by email without undue delay (Art. 16(4)). The form also shows you a reference number.
2. **Review.** A person on our team reviews the report against the law and our Terms. We do not use automated tools to decide on reports.
3. **Decision.** We decide in a timely, diligent and objective manner. We may remove the document, restrict access to it (it is then private and no longer reachable by link), suspend or terminate the account behind it, or take no action. We may contact the owner of the content to hear their side first.
4. **Decision notice.** We email you what we decided, the reasons and how you can seek redress (Art. 16(5)). If you reported child sexual abuse material without an email address, we cannot send you these notices.

We aim to act within **24 hours** on reports of child sexual abuse material and imminent threats to life or safety, within **3 business days** on other clear-cut reports, and within **14 calendar days** on reports that need a closer legal assessment.

## What we act on

We remove or restrict content that:

- is illegal under Swiss law, EU law or the law of an EU member state;
- infringes intellectual property rights;
- is child sexual abuse material, which we always report to the authorities;
- contains threats, targeted harassment or doxxing;
- is phishing, malware or fraud, or otherwise breaks our acceptable-use rules.

We do not remove content just because someone disagrees with it, nor lawful criticism, reporting or satire. Disputes between collaborators about a workspace document are for the workspace's admins to resolve.

## If your content is affected

If we remove or restrict your content or suspend your account, we send you a **statement of reasons** (Art. 17) by email, if your account has an email address on file. It explains:

- what we did and, if applicable, for how long;
- the facts we relied on, including whether we acted on a report or on our own initiative;
- the legal provision or the clause of our Terms the decision is based on;
- that the decision was taken by a person, not by an automated system;
- how you can object.

**Anonymous documents.** An anonymous document has no contact details. We cannot send a statement of reasons to its creator, and we record the statement as undeliverable. We keep the text of every notice we send, or could not deliver, with the report.

**How to object.** You can complain to **{{abuseContactEmail}}** within **six months** of our decision. Reply to the statement of reasons or quote its reference, and explain why the decision was wrong. A person reviews your complaint, and we restore the content or the account if the decision was wrong. We tell you the outcome. You can also turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body, and you always have the right to go to court. Reporters can use the same channel to object to a decision not to act on their report.

## Threats to life or safety

If we become aware of information giving rise to a suspicion that a criminal offence involving a threat to the life or safety of a person has taken place, is taking place or is likely to take place, we promptly inform the law-enforcement or judicial authorities of the EU member state concerned, or Europol if we cannot identify that state, and the Swiss police where Switzerland is concerned (Art. 18). We give them all relevant information we have. This includes, but is not limited to, child sexual abuse material and threats of violence or terrorism.

## Orders from authorities

If a competent authority orders us to act against illegal content or to provide information, we follow the order as required by law and inform the user concerned unless the authority asks us not to.

## Misuse of the reporting channel

We may stop processing reports from people who frequently submit manifestly unfounded reports, after a warning and for a reasonable period. Knowingly false reports may also break our Terms (§6).

## Data we process for reports

To handle a report we store the address of the reported content, your explanation, the category, your name and email address (unless you left them out of a report of child sexual abuse material), the language you used, when you confirmed the good-faith statement and the time, plus a shortened IP address and a hash of your browser's user agent against report flooding. For reports sent while signed in, we also link your account. We keep a report, with the notices about it, while the case is open and delete it 1 year after it is closed. Your data export contains the reports you filed while signed in. See the Privacy Policy ([/legal/privacy](/legal/privacy)) for details.
